## Recovery boundaries

The approved fallback restores matching PostgreSQL, binaries, configuration and
units; binary-only daemon rollback onto the expanded schema is unsupported.
Prefer reviewed roll-forward fixes. A restore must preserve the failed database
and reconcile post-backup submissions, artifacts and provider effects before
traffic resumes.

The existing `pg-backup.sh` validates archive shape and prunes its selected backup
directory. Keep the rollout recovery package separately from routine retention.
`pg-restore-check.sh --no-migrate EXPLICIT_BACKUP` restores into a scratch database
and checks relationships, but does **not** prove v35 migration compatibility or
retained daemon startup. Do not count that script alone as the complete drill.

follow-ups, not v35 closure prerequisites under the October 19 rescope.
